## JobMatch AI – Intelligent Internship Matching and Eligibility Assistant

**Description:** Upload a resume and internship descriptions to identify suitable internships, evaluate eligibility, and assess skill compatibility.

**Core Features:**
- Extract candidate skills, education, projects, and experience.
- Analyse internship requirements.
- Verify internship eligibility.
- Calculate candidate-internship matching scores.
- Identify missing skills and qualifications.
- Recommend suitable internships.

**AI Workflow:**
Resume Extraction → Internship Requirement Extraction → Eligibility Verification → Skill Matching → Match Scoring → Skill-Gap Analysis → Internship Recommendations
